Design Integrity

Clear, calm guidelines that protect every neighbour's view.

Klipdrift architecture favours timeless natural materials over fleeting trends. Homes stay below the valley ridgeline, use muted earth tones, dressed sandstone, and dark corrugated or slate roofs, and conceal boundary fences within indigenous planting.
