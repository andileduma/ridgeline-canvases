What Sets Klipdrift Apart

A residential sanctuary first, graced by a great golf course.

Unlike busy public resorts where residents compete with tournament crowds, Klipdrift separates member hospitality from quiet residential enclaves. Over sixty percent of the estate remains open fairway, riverine grassland, and protected wetland.

01

Valley Links Landscape

Eighteen flowing holes shaped around natural sandstone ridges, spring-fed dams, and indigenous river corridors.

02

Restrained Sandstone Architecture

Homes use local dressed sandstone, seasoned timber screens, and deep shaded stoeps that sit quietly below the treeline.

03

Long-Term Estate Stewardship

An independent homeowners association governs course agronomy, wetland conservation, and architectural standards with transparent annual levies.
